# English Improved

A clarity pass on Gunfire Reborn's English text. The goal is to explain what triggers an effect, who receives it, how it changes gameplay, and how stacks or resources are gained and spent.

## Coverage

- Hero Primary and Secondary Skills and supporting explanations.
- Occult Scroll descriptions, including enhanced and alternate-mode versions.
- Ascensions, permanent Talents, and Spiritual Blessings.
- Weapon descriptions and normal, rare, exclusive, and Gemini Inscriptions.
- Status effects, combat help, and mechanic explanations.
- Seasonal systems: Arcane Ascensions, devices, Demon Spirits, Spiritual Links, Talismans/Power Gems, Dice, Rune Stones/Runic Energy, and Soul Pendants/Soul Jades.
- Associated mode-upgrade descriptions, including Spiritual Assault modules.

Many names, menus, achievements, challenges, and lore entries retain the game's original English. Coverage includes both substantial rewrites and lighter terminology/grammar cleanup.

## Terminology

Named modifiers such as **All DMG**, **Total Weapon DMG**, **Total Skill DMG**, and **Final DMG** are retained and generally bolded when they identify a calculation mechanic. The game's terminology is inconsistent: “total damage taken” can also mean a sum of recent damage, as in Safety Measures, where it is unbolded.

Descriptions use explicit subjects and conditions. Examples include Leaf Spirits losing their own HP, Spirit Summon's base conversion limit of one seed per cast, and Wild Punch applying while Tidal Aspis is active. Jackpot's random-draw Inscription explicitly refers to its weapon bonus effects.

## Limitations

Wording edits do not alter gameplay. Some source descriptions omit probabilities, formulas, or timing details. Their values and uncertainties are retained rather than replaced with guesses. Not every tooltip's layout or interaction has been verified in-game.

The included file is the same version installed and reviewed during the English Improved editing project. Future game updates may require merging new keys and reviewing changed effects.
